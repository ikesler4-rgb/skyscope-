    Satellite-only test

    Your iPhone mixes satellites with Wi-Fi and cell towers. Indoors, Wi-Fi alone can look decent. To measure satellites only:

    1. Open SkyScope and tap Start while you still have internet.
    2. Turn on Airplane Mode. GPS keeps working, Wi-Fi and cell positioning stop.
    3. Wait 30 seconds. The reading now comes from satellites alone.

    iPhones don't share individual satellite data with any app. SkyScope measures what Apple does provide: the reported accuracy and how much your position wanders.
